About Magnum

Transforming Low-Grade Minerals, Metals, Alloys, Slags and Industrial Waste

Magnum Ore Industry's supplied company profile states that the company was established in Nagpur, India, in 2004.

About the Company

Magnum Ore Industry describes itself as an industrial processing company specializing in mineral beneficiation, metal recovery, slag recycling and industrial waste management.


Its stated approach is to transform low-grade minerals, metals, alloys, slags and industrial waste into valuable resources and products while supporting sustainable manufacturing and circular economy initiatives.

Our Vision

To build a durable and economical mineral, metal and slag processing ecosystem by supplying advanced beneficiation plants and machinery, transforming mining and industrial waste into valuable resources while promoting environmental responsibility and industrial growth.

Our Mission

  • Maximize utilization of low-grade minerals, metals, alloys and slags.
  • Reduce industrial pollution.
  • Support Make in India initiatives.
  • Create employment and skill development opportunities.
  • Promote circular economy solutions for industrial sustainability.
